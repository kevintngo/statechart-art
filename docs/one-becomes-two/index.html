What TLC found

  • Holds: a cell never divides before it copies its DNA, and a cell never gets stuck.
  • Breaks without p53: damaged DNA gets copied, and both daughters carry the mutation (a path of 11 steps).
  • Breaks without the spindle check: the sisters are pulled apart too soon, and the daughters get the wrong chromosomes (a path of 9 steps).
